Harbor Atelier — open example

Fictional awards sizzle for the skill. No client media, no paid-gen stills, no files under src. Pattern only. See README.md. 45s, hard-cut primary, one whip accent. Payoff about every 2–4s. Claude’s comparison cut is the density bar this pattern is practicing: real pictures plus a named type recipe, not a fade card.

Direction

Message
One night in their room, told with their pictures.
Arc
Hook (OPEN) → Build (STUDIO, EXPAND) → Punch (POWER, TAKEOVER) → Resolve (END)
Audience
An awards jury and the partners on the end card.
Mood
Medium energy, hard cuts, one whip.
Two-color
Ground #0c0e12, ink #f4efe6. Accent only on emphasis words.
Breather
07 — interview b-roll. Held read, captions, no second animation.
Callback
The door still in 01 returns as the closing picture in 13.
Spectacle
05 — 42 PIECES, spring-scale-in on the real install photo. The one exaggeration.
Negatives
No fade-card titles, no grey selects, no invented UI, no corner labels, no glitch default, no Flashtalk or interview TEMP, no HYPERFRAMES_SKIP_SKILLS, no paid gen.
Seams
hard cut. Accent: 09 whip-pan into takeover
